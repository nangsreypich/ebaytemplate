Product Features

High-Torque

Engineered with an ultra-powerful engine that delivers exceptional torque over long operating intervals. This ensures your operators maintain maximum digging force, structural breakout stability, and superior lifting capacities even under the most demanding site environments and dense clay conditions.

Safety Certified

Maximize on-site productivity safely with our fully certified overhead guard protection setups and advanced quick-attach structural coupler frameworks. Designed for lightning-fast tool changes, this mechanism allows field operators to pivot smoothly between heavy tasks without risking crew downtime.

Quick-Attach

Optimized for compact urban jobsites where maneuvering workspace parameters are strictly limited. This nimble infrastructure delivers a reduced overall physical operating footprint without compromising structural force or hydraulic lifting power, allowing hassle-free travel paths.

Every load-bearing hinge node point incorporates oversized accessible grease fittings to ensure effortless field servicing and maintenance workflows.
